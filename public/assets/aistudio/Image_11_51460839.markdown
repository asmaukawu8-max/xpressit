## Brand & Style

This design system powers a modern, safe, and emotionally resonant mobile sanctuary where vulnerability meets discretion. The brand voice balances total anonymity with genuine human warmth: introspective, reassuring, modern, and non-judgmental. It avoids the harsh, chaotic darkness often associated with anonymous forums in favor of an airy, radiant, and therapeutic sanctuary.

The visual style blends **Soft Modernism** with **Tactile Fluidity**:
- **Radiant Surfaces**: Pure white cards float over warm, calming off-white backgrounds, framed with ultra-fine, hairline borders to maintain structure without rigid visual noise.
- **Expressive Micro-Moments**: Tactile pill shapes, luminous indigo accents, and emotive coral bursts create an inviting, safe space for candid storytelling.
- **Generous Breathing Room**: Expansive vertical rhythms and airy padding encourage unhurried, comfortable reading of deep personal reflections.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around emotional safety and high legibility:

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Deep Indigo)**: Anchors identity, navigation, and key conversion paths. Represents calm depth, trust, and introspection. Tonal variants range from `#6366F1` (interactive/active states) down to `#4338CA` (pressed/focused states).
- **Secondary (`#EEF2FF` - Soft Lavender Mist)**: Serves as low-intensity container fills, category tag backdrops, and active-state pill highlights. It cushions primary interactions with gentle contrast. Mid-tones like `#E0E7FF` and `#C7D2FE` designate subtle borders and active indicators.
- **Tertiary (`#F43F5E` - Radiant Rose / Coral)**: Reserved strictly for heart/empathy reactions, confession highlights, active recording indicators, and high-impact emotional signals. Paired with `#FB7185` for soft glows and hover/active states.
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: The default canvas sits at `#FAFAFC` (Warm Studio White) transitioning to `#F3F4F6` for grouped backgrounds. Interactive and narrative cards use `#FFFFFF` to ensure complete contrast and optical lift. Hairline dividers use `#E2E8F0`.
- **Text & Hierarchy**:
  - Primary Copy: `#0F172A` (Rich Slate/Charcoal) for effortless reading.
  - Secondary Copy: `#334155` for contextual prompts and metadata.
  - Muted/Timestamp Copy: `#64748B` for secondary labels, counters, and placeholders.

## Typography

Typography prioritizes long-form emotional legibility and casual intimacy using **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Generous Vertical Cadence**: Long anonymous confessions and lived experiences are prone to visual fatigue. `body-lg` and `body-md` employ extended line-height ratios (~1.6xâ€“1.65x) to create a relaxed, open reading plane.
- **Expressive Headlines**: Large titles utilize tighter tracking (-0.02em to -0.03em) and bold/extrabold weights to convey decisive presence without feeling industrial or technical.
- **Micro-Labels**: Badges, pseudonymous handles, and interaction counters use semi-bold weights (`label-md` and `label-sm`) with slight positive tracking (+0.01em to +0.03em) for sharp legibility on high-density mobile screens.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined **4px / 8px incremental grid**, scaled fluidly for native mobile and responsive viewports:

- **Mobile Viewport (Primary)**: 
  - Standard edge canvas margin: `1rem` (16px), expanding to `1.25rem` (20px) on large phablet viewports.
  - Inter-card feed spacing: `space-md` (16px) vertical stack.
  - Safe-area compensation: Floating elements enforce a persistent bottom offset of `5.5rem` (88px) to clear the floating bottom navigation and the OS Home Indicator.
- **Card Padding**:
  - Story feed cards: `space-lg` (24px) internal padding, ensuring deep breathing room around long narrative bodies.
  - Compact cards/Comments: `space-md` (16px) internal padding.
- **Grouped Density**:
  - Reaction badges, metadata pills, and anonymous avatar groupings maintain tight inline gaps (`space-xs` = 4px to `space-sm` = 8px) to read as cohesive functional clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows, opting instead for **multi-layered, chromatic ambient diffusion**:

- **Level 0 (Flat Canvas)**: `#FAFAFC` baseline.
- **Level 1 (Card Rest State)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and an ambient glow:
  - `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05);`
- **Level 2 (Interactive Drag / Active Card)**: Used during swipe gestures or feed focus:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.04), 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08);`
- **Level 3 (Floating Bottom Nav & Overlays)**: High-altitude elements that skim above content:
  - Backing: 80% opacity `#FFFFFF` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Level 4 (Floating Center Action Button)**:
  - Background: Gradient from `#6366F1` to `#4F46E5`.
  - Shadow: Tinted brand aura: `0 8px 20px -2px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.38), 0 2px 6px rgba(79, 70, 229, 0.24)`.

## Shapes

The geometric vocabulary expresses comfort, safety, and non-rigidity:

- **Cards & Story Containers**: Rounded with generous radii (`16px` for small list items, `20px` to `24px` for main story feeds and confession containers).
- **Interactive Controls & Meta**: Full capsule / pill radii (`9999px`) are standard for category badges, anonymous avatar frames, tags, filters, and audio playheads.
- **Inputs & Textareas**: `16px` rounded corners for rich text inputs and confession creation fields, creating an inviting, notebook-like tactile canvas.
- **Dialogs & Bottom Sheets**: Top corners rounded to `28px` to present an approachable, organic sheet transition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Full-pill (`rounded-full`), solid `#4F46E5` fill with `#FFFFFF` text, bold `label-lg`. Micro-elevation tinted shadow. On press: `#4338CA`.
- **Secondary**: Soft Lavender `#EEF2FF` fill with `#4F46E5` text. On press: `#E0E7FF`.
- **Emotional/Empathy**: `#FFF1F2` fill with `#F43F5E` text and icon.

### Anonymous Avatar Chips
- Circular container (`36px` to `44px`) displaying randomized geometric or gradient art masks (e.g., Violet-to-Rose or Lavender-to-Indigo blends).
- Accompanied by generated whimsical pseudonyms (e.g., "Thoughtful Sparrow", "Midnight Poet") set in `label-md` `#0F172A` with an adjacent relative timestamp in `label-sm` `#64748B`.

### Category & Topic Pills
- Height: `32px`. Rounded: `9999px`.
- Padding: `0 14px`.
- Default: `#F1F5F9` background, `#334155` text (`label-md`).
- Selected: `#4F46E5` background, `#FFFFFF` text, or subtle `#EEF2FF` fill with `#4338CA` text and `#C7D2FE` border.

### Reaction Badges
- Capsule pills (`28px` height) with a hairline border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) over white or subtle tinted fills.
